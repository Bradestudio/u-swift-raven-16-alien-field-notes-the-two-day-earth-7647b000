## The line

> Three stars: beautiful oceans, but the locals apologise to furniture when they bump into it. Polite planet, baffling priorities.

128 characters. Why this line wins, against the judge's five criteria:

1. **Reads like an alien tourist's one-line review of Earth** — the visitor's outside view is the whole sentence ("the locals", "this planet"), with a star rating up front and a closing verdict, like a real tourist review.
2. **Funny from the alien angle** — the laugh comes from a visitor watching humans say sorry to a chair. The premise *is* the joke; nothing is bolted on.
3. **Original** — apologising to furniture is a small, specific, observed detail, not traffic, gravity, money or weather, the first obvious thoughts every other entrant reaches for.
4. **Sounds like a real review** — stars, then a verdict: "Polite planet, baffling priorities."
5. **Tight** — one joke, landed once, punch at the end, nothing explained.
